💡 Teaching Philosophy

"Teaching is not just about transferring knowledge, but debugging mindsets and inspiring curiosity."

My philosophy centers on active learning and having fun while doing it. I believe the best classroom is an inclusive space where asking "silly" questions is encouraged, mistakes are just part of the process, and students learn by actually doing rather than just listening.
