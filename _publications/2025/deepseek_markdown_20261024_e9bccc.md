title: "Practitioners’ expectations on log anomaly detection"
selected: true
pub: "IEEE Transactions on Software Engineering (TSE)"
pub_date: "2025"
abstract: >-
  This paper presents an empirical study surveying 312 practitioners from 36 countries to uncover their expectations and adoption factors regarding log anomaly detection. By comparing these needs with a decade of premier research (2015-2025), the study highlights the gaps between academic focus and industrial requirements, providing clear directions for future research to better meet practitioners' needs.
