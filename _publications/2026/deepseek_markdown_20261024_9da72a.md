title: "AI-supported scenario-based learning in entrepreneurship education: A comparative classroom study of learning processes and outcomes"
selected: true
pub: "International Journal of Management Education"
pub_date: "2026"
abstract: >-
  This study examines how AI-supported scenario-based learning shapes student outcomes in entrepreneurship education. Using a quasi-experimental design with 42 students, we compared regular instruction, static AI interaction, and a multi-agent dynamic simulation. Findings indicate that the multi-agent dynamic simulation yielded stronger gains in self-efficacy, opportunity competence, and decision quality. The results suggest that the value of AI depends on the broader instructional design rather than technology alone.
