title: "Advancing diagram-based reasoning in AI tutoring systems: a structural approach for STEM education"
selected: true
pub: "Smart Learning Environments"
pub_date: "2026"
abstract: >-
  To address the challenges AI tutoring systems face in interpreting complex STEM diagrams, this paper proposes StructRAG, a framework combining visual parsing with pattern-level structural reasoning. By integrating OCR, large language models, and graph pattern retrieval, StructRAG identifies missing connections and provides structure-aware feedback. Evaluated on 1,650 STEM questions, it achieves an 89.3% question-level accuracy and a 93.0% edge-level F1 score, significantly outperforming existing baselines and demonstrating strong potential for complex diagram assessments.
