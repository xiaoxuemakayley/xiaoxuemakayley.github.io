title: "Trusting AI to detect AI? A systematic evaluation of the reliability and robustness of current AIGC detection tools for student academic work"
selected: true
pub: "Computers & Education"
pub_date: "2026"
abstract: >-
  This study constructs three large-scale ecological datasets (280,000+ samples) to systematically evaluate 13 mainstream AIGC detectors in authentic educational contexts. The results reveal systematic failures in engineering code and short-form tasks, significant algorithmic bias in STEM disciplines, and extreme vulnerability to adversarial attacks (up to 88% evasion rate). The findings suggest current detection technologies are inadequate for high-stakes assessments, highlighting the need for future detectors to evolve toward "logical innovation recognition" within human-AI collaboration frameworks.
