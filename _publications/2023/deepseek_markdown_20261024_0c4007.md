title: "A Semi-supervised Approach for Industrial Anomaly Detection via Self-adaptive Clustering"
selected: true
pub: "IEEE Transactions on Industrial Informatics (TII)"
pub_date: "2023"
abstract: >-
  This paper proposes AdaLog, an integrated semi-supervised approach based on self-adaptive clustering for industrial anomaly detection. By employing automatic label probability estimation, a pretrained model for contextual learning, and an undersampling method to alleviate class imbalance, AdaLog significantly outperforms state-of-the-art semi-supervised and supervised baselines in F1-score across three popular datasets.
